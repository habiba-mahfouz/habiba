ABOUT ME

Since I was young, I've loved anything that feels special from drawing to crochet — something where you can see a reflection of your own soul in it. Even when I'm picking out a gift for someone, it can't just be "anything" — it has to feel like them.

When I started learning Front end, I realized that's exactly the feeling I wanted to build into websites too: sites that actually look like the people or brands behind them, not just another generic design that could belong to anyone.

If you want a website that actually feels like you, not just another template — you're in the right place.
